# EduBand — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 August 2026**

EduBand ("we", "the app") helps students practise and improve spoken
communication. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you
remove it. It is written to be readable by students and parents, not only by
lawyers.

> **Publish this at a public URL** (e.g. GitHub Pages) and paste that URL into
> Play Console → App content → Privacy policy. Replace `CONTACT_EMAIL` below
> with a real address you monitor before publishing.

---

## 1. Who we are

EduBand is operated by the EduBand team. For any privacy question or request,
contact **CONTACT_EMAIL**.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Why | Kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Voice recordings** | To transcribe and analyse your speaking | Deleted immediately after analysis by default. Kept only if you switch "Keep raw audio" on in Privacy settings. |
| **Transcripts & scores** | To show your report and track growth over time | Until you delete them |
| **Name, level, school/college code** | To personalise the app and group school users | Until you delete your account |
| **Email address** | To sign you in and secure your account | Until you delete your account |
| **Parental/guardian consent record** | Legally required before recording a minor | Until you delete your data |

We do **not** collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or
device fingerprints.

## 3. Microphone

The app records **only** while you are on the Record screen and have tapped the
record button. There is no background or passive listening. Recording is
blocked entirely until a parent/guardian consent record exists.

## 4. How your data is used

- To produce your communication feedback (transcription, speech metrics, scoring).
- To show your progress over time.
- Nothing else. **We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party
  advertising or tracking SDKs.**

## 5. Who we share it with

We use these processors purely to deliver the service:

- **Supabase** — authentication and encrypted database/storage hosting.
- **Speech-to-text provider** (Deepgram or AssemblyAI) — converts your audio to text.
- **AI scoring provider** (Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini) — generates the feedback.

Audio and transcripts are sent to these providers only to process your session.
They are not used to build advertising profiles. We share data with no one else,
except where legally required.

## 6. Children's privacy

EduBand is designed for students, including minors.

- A **parent or guardian must grant consent before any recording is possible.**
- We collect the minimum data needed to provide the service.
- No behavioural advertising is served to any user, of any age.
- Parents/guardians may request access to, or deletion of, their child's data at
  any time via **CONTACT_EMAIL**.

## 7. Your controls

Inside the app, under **Privacy & data**, you can at any time:

- **Export my data** — download a full JSON copy.
- **Delete all my data** — permanently erase your profile, consent record, and
  every session, on the device and on our servers. This cannot be undone.
- **Keep raw audio** — off by default; controls whether recordings survive analysis.

## 8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored encrypted at rest by our hosting
provider. Database access is protected by row-level security so a user can only
read their own data; teachers and parents see only records explicitly shared
with them.

## 9. Legal basis and your rights

We process data on the basis of consent (and, for minors, verifiable
parental/guardian consent). You have the right to access, correct, export, and
delete your data, and to withdraw consent at any time — withdrawing consent
stops all further recording.

This policy is designed around India's **DPDP Act 2023** and is configurable for
**GDPR / GDPR-K**, **COPPA**, and **FERPA**. It is informational and is not
legal advice.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the "Last updated" date and
notify users in the app.
